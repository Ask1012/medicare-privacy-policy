MediCare – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

MediCare is a web-based academic project designed to help patients and their guardians manage medicine schedules and receive medicine reminders.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how information is handled when using the MediCare application.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how MediCare is configured and used, the application may collect the following information:

  • Patient name and basic profile information.
  • Guardian or parent contact information.
  • Patient WhatsApp phone number.
  • Medicine names and dosage information.
  • Medicine schedules and instructions.
  • Medicine reminder and medication status information.
  • Date and time information related to medicine schedules.
  • Responses to medicine reminders, such as Taken or Remind Later.

2. How Information Is Used

Information collected by MediCare may be used to:

  • Create and manage patient medicine schedules.
  • Send medicine reminders.
  • Record medication status.
  • Notify an authorized guardian when applicable.
  • Generate medication reports.
  • Improve and demonstrate the academic project.

3. WhatsApp Communications

MediCare may use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to send medicine reminder messages and receive responses from users.

For example, a patient may receive a medicine reminder containing the medicine name, dosage, instructions, and scheduled time. A user may respond using available reminder buttons such as "Taken" or "Remind Later".

WhatsApp messages are processed through Meta's WhatsApp services. Users should also review the applicable privacy information and terms provided by Meta and WhatsApp.

4. Data Storage

MediCare is designed to store application information in a MySQL database. Information may include patient records, medicine schedules, reminder records, and medication logs.

The exact information stored depends on the configuration and usage of the academic application.

5. Sharing of Information

MediCare does not intend to sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary for functionality. In particular, WhatsApp messaging functionality may use Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Information may also be accessible to authorized administrators of the MediCare application for legitimate application-management purposes.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as it is required for the operation, testing, demonstration, or academic evaluation of the MediCare project.

Data retention periods may vary depending on the application's configuration.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures may be used to protect information handled by the application. However, MediCare is an academic project and should not be considered a substitute for a professionally audited healthcare information system.

Users should avoid submitting information that is not necessary for the intended functionality of the application.

8. Third-Party Services

MediCare may use third-party services such as:

  • Meta WhatsApp Business Platform for WhatsApp messaging.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting this privacy policy webpage.

These third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies.

9. User Rights and Requests

Users who want to ask about information associated with their MediCare account or request correction or deletion should contact the administrator or project owner responsible for the MediCare application.

10. Children's Privacy

MediCare is not specifically designed as a service for children. The application should not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the MediCare application or its data-handling practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the MediCare academic project, please contact the project administrator.
