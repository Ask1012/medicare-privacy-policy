MediCare – Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

1. Requesting Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their MediCare account or patient record.

To request deletion, contact the administrator responsible for the MediCare academic project and provide enough information to identify the relevant account or patient record.

2. Information That May Be Deleted

Depending on the application configuration, a deletion request may include:

  • Patient profile information.
  • Guardian contact information.
  • WhatsApp phone number associated with the record.
  • Medicine information.
  • Medicine schedules.
  • Medication logs.
  • Reminder and notification records.

3. Processing a Deletion Request

After receiving a valid request, the project administrator will review the request and identify the relevant information stored by the MediCare application.

Information that is eligible for deletion will be removed from the application's active records where reasonably possible.

4. Third-Party Services

MediCare may use third-party services such as Meta WhatsApp Business Platform. Information processed by those services may be subject to their own retention and deletion policies.

Users may need to contact the relevant third-party service separately for information held directly by that service.

5. Academic Project Records

Because MediCare is an academic project, limited information may be retained where necessary for project evaluation, documentation, testing, or demonstration, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Contact

For a data deletion request or questions about deletion, contact the administrator responsible for the MediCare project.
